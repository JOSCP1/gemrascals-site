Privacy Policy

Gem Rascals · Last updated 5 November 2026

SupportImpressum

Summary

Gem Rascals does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. The game has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet to play. The only optional purchase is handled by the app store you downloaded the game from.

Data the app uses

Data collected by the app

None. Gem Rascals does not transmit any information from your device to us or to anyone else.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device only: your settings (sound, music, language and the on/off switch for the helper), the name you type for the local highscore list, which world you are playing, your saved game, and your local highscore list. This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

In-app purchase (the Premium pack)

Gem Rascals offers one optional purchase, the Premium pack. The payment is processed entirely by the app store you use — the App Store (Apple) on iPhone and iPad, or the Amazon Appstore on Fire tablets and where the app is installed from Amazon — under that company’s own privacy policy. We never receive your payment details or any personal data; the app only learns whether the pack has been bought, so it can unlock the extra content. On Amazon devices the app uses Amazon’s In-App Purchasing component to check this; it runs only when you open the Premium page or make or restore a purchase.

Clipboard

When you tap “Copy code”, “Paste” or “Share” for a world code, the app copies that short code to, or reads it from, your device’s clipboard, or opens the system share sheet. This happens only when you tap those buttons, and only the short world-code text is involved.

Permissions

Gem Rascals does not ask for access to your location, health data, contacts, microphone, camera, motion data or any other protected information.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising networks, no analytics services and no tracking software. The only third-party component is the app store’s own in-app-purchase support described above (Apple StoreKit on iOS, Amazon’s In-App Purchasing on Amazon devices), used solely to sell the Premium pack.

App stores

When you download the app or make a purchase, the app store (Apple, Amazon or Google) processes that transaction under its own privacy policy. The store may also send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics, but only if you have agreed to share them with app developers in your device settings. We cannot identify you from this information.

Children

Gem Rascals is designed to be suitable for children. Because the app collects no personal data, it also collects none from children. The only purchase is placed behind a parental gate (a question intended for a grown-up) before it can be started, and the game contains no links out to other apps or websites.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. When you visit it, the hosting provider (GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA) automatically processes technical data such as your IP address, the date and time of the request and your browser type in server log files. This is necessary to deliver the website securely (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). We do not combine this data with other sources.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to access, correct, delete and restrict the processing of your personal data, the right to data portability and the right to object. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. As the app stores no personal data with us, we normally hold no data about you.
